## 2026-10-02: citation pass

Every dated historical claim in `docs/chapters/` was checked against the page cited for it, and the
citation added inline. Each MacTutor page was fetched and the specific sentence supporting the
claim was read; authorship and "last update" dates in `references.bib` are copied from the page.

Corrections made during the pass:

- **Construction of ℝ** (Ch 6): "both due, independently, to Cantor and Dedekind" → cuts by
  Dedekind (1872); Cauchy-sequence constructions by Cantor and Heine (1872), anticipated by Méray
  (1869). Source: MacTutor, *The real numbers: Stevin to Hilbert*.
- **Weierstrass** (Ch 17, timeline): "1870s" → from his 1859/60 Berlin lectures. Source: MacTutor
  biography.
- **Galois** (Ch 8, map): "1830s" → "around 1830" (he died in 1832). Source: MacTutor biography.
- **"Ring"** (Ch 11): the term is Hilbert's (MacTutor, *Ring Theory*); the earlier text tied it to
  the 1897 *Zahlbericht*, which MacTutor does not state. Reworded.
- **"Körper" 1871** (Ch 11): MacTutor's biographies do not date it; Jeff Miller's *Earliest Known
  Uses* (hosted by MacTutor) does: "due, in concrete form, to Dedekind in 1871".
- **Hilbert's program "around 1920"** (Ch 5): SEP, *Hilbert's Program* (Zach, rev. 2023): "proposed
  his program in this form only in 1921".
- **Timeline figure**: boxes reordered so the arrows follow the dates.
